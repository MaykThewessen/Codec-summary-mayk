Which codec wins,
and exactly where

Every codec argument is the same argument: how much of the original are you willing to throw away, and how many decoders do you need to survive. Each map below places the real formats on those two axes and names the winner for every square. The numbers come from sweeps run in this repository, not from vendor claims.

Why these agree with each other

One method, five domains

The maps share a shape because they share a discipline. Four rules did most of the work, and each of them changed a headline number at least once.

Compare at matched quality, not matched settings

JPEG q80 and AVIF q80 are unrelated numbers, and so are HEVC CRF 23 and AV1 CRF 23. Every codec is swept on its own ladder, scored with a perceptual metric, then interpolated to a fixed quality target before anything is compared.

Never let the sweep decide the answer

If one codec's ladder stops earlier than another's, the comparison at that end measures your sweep, not the codec. Extending the AVIF ladder moved the images crossover from 83 to 87; xz -6 and -9 turned out to emit byte-identical output until the corpus grew past their dictionary; and the video ladders had to be set per clip, because two clips eight CRF steps apart would otherwise have made the result an artefact.

Generate every sentence containing a number

Numeric prose is derived from the measurement files at build time rather than typed, so re-running a sweep cannot leave the text asserting something the charts no longer show.

Say what could not be measured

VVC, RAR, HE-AAC and AVIF lossless have no usable encoder in this environment. They appear as explicitly tagged cited-or-absent entries, never as a plausible-looking number.

Scripts, raw sweeps and derived analysis live in the repository beside these pages, so every figure can be regenerated or argued with. Each map is a single self-contained file: fonts and data inlined, nothing fetched at runtime.

docs/ holds the built pages, scripts/<domain>/ the measurement code, data/<domain>/ the results.
